1. Why are the variables declared as private?

The variables are declared as private to protect the student's data from being accessed or modified directly from outside the Student class. This follows the concept of encapsulation.

2. What happens if the variables are declared public?

If the variables are public, other classes can access and change them directly. This reduces data security and breaks encapsulation.

3. Why do we use getters and setters?

Getters are used to retrieve private variable values, while setters are used to update them in a controlled way. They provide safe access to private data.
